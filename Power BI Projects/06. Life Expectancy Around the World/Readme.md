# Life Expectancy Around the World

## Objective

Explore global life expectancy trends from 1950 to 2021, comparing regions, genders, and age groups to understand shifts in human longevity.

## Data Source

Data sourced from [Our World in Data (OWID)](https://ourworldindata.org/life-expectancy).

| File | Records | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| `life_expectancy.csv` | 20,755 | Life expectancy by country/entity from 1950-2021 |
| `life_expectancy_female_male.csv` | -- | Life expectancy split by gender |
| `life_expectancy_different_ages.csv` | -- | Life expectancy at different ages (birth, 10, 25, etc.) |

## Key Questions

- Do women live longer than men in Africa, and how does that compare to the rest of the world?
- What are the major shifts in global life expectancy over the decades?
- How does Africa compare to the developed world for life expectancy at birth vs. at age 10?
- Which countries have seen the most dramatic improvements?
